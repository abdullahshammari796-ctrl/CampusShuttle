3. How the sizes were reached
   * Sources (no element, attribute, operation, association, multiplicity or message removed or renamed):
     - stereotype labels of the class diagrams 08, 09, 12–16 enlarged from 12 px to 13 px
       (skinparam classStereotypeFontSize 13);
     - guard texts of A.5's fragments enlarged from 11 px to 13 px (<style> sequenceDiagram { group { FontSize 13 } });
     - A.5's participant names and its five longest messages broken over several lines: 1,799 → 1,365 px wide.
   * Layout (scripts/build-docs.ts, UML-set PDF only): where overlapping detail pages could not reach 7 pt, the
     diagram is split recursively along empty lines that no text, box or class crosses ("coherent details"). Where a
     class diagram has no such line, overlapping page windows each show whole classes, and a class cut by a window's
     edge is faded on that page. The overview page outlines and numbers each detail. A.6 now gets a full page of its
     own instead of a 6.9 pt page shared with its heading.
