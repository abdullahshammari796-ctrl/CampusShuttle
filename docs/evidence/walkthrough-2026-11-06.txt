DEFECTS FOUND AND FIXED (test plan item 17)
A. Forecast heat-map cells (UC5 step 3) were <td> elements with a click handler only: not focusable and not
   exposed as controls, so the explanation could not be opened with the keyboard.
B. An automated scan of the office page (never scanned before) found WCAG 1.4.3 contrast failures on
   heat-map cells and, at 390 px, scrollable tables that no key could reach. Details:
   docs/evidence/accessibility-office-2026-11-06.txt.

OBSERVATIONS NOT CHANGED (for the team's review)
1. Background tabs: the driver tab stopped sending simulated positions at 23:44:51, about five minutes after
   it was last in front, and resumed at 23:45:31 when brought to the front. Browsers throttle timers in hidden
   pages; the server correctly showed "Location delayed" meanwhile. For the live demonstration, keep the driver
   window visible (separate windows, not background tabs). A phone with its screen off may behave the same way
   (report §6.5).
2. The student list showed "Now" while a shuttle was 357 m (about 50 s) away: minutes are rounded down.
3. Near a minute boundary the driver's next-stop estimate alternated between "1 min" and "2 min" for about
   20 s (23:48:27–23:48:47): the local countdown and each new server estimate disagree by a few seconds.
4. The Track and End buttons in the office tables have the same accessible name in every row; a screen-reader
   user must use table navigation to know which trip a button belongs to.
