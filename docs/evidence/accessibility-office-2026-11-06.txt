Office page accessibility — automated checks, 6–7 November 2026 (test plan item 17)

METHOD
axe-core 4.14.0 through @axe-core/playwright 4.14.0, tags wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21aa and wcag22aa, on all five
office tabs (Live operations, Demand & forecast, Routes, Trip history, System), light and dark colour schemes,
at 1280 × 900 and 390 × 844, against the walkthrough server with demonstration data (Playwright 1.64.1,
Chrome for Testing 156, Windows 11, Node 24.14.1). These are automated checks: they are not a screen-reader
session, a physical-device check or WCAG certification.

BEFORE THE FIX (main at 695eb88)
- Demand & forecast, every size and scheme: color-contrast (serious). White numbers on the mid-blue heat-map
  cell #3987e5 (about 3.6:1; small text needs 4.5:1); 20 cells in light mode and 33 in dark mode. In dark mode
  the ramp colour #2a78d6 reached only 4.4:1 with white and 4.2:1 with the dark ink.
- Demand & forecast at 390 px: scrollable-region-focusable (serious), 3 table wrappers.
- System at 390 px, once the audit log had a real entry: scrollable-region-focusable (serious), 1 wrapper.
- Keyboard: the forecast heat-map cells were <td> elements with a click handler only. Tab never reached them,
  so the explanation (UC5 step 3) could not be opened without a mouse.
- No horizontal page overflow on any tab at either width.

FIX
- public/js/office.js: each forecast cell holds a <button> with an accessible name, e.g.
  "Library & Learning Commons 08:00: 8 expected".
- public/js/charts.js: the ink colour is chosen by computed WCAG contrast (the higher of two inks); the dark-ramp
  colour #2a78d6 was replaced by #2672cc, so every ramp colour reaches 4.5:1 with one ink.
- public/office.html: the history, backtest and audit table wrappers are focusable, named regions; the
  explanation area is aria-live="polite".
- public/css/app.css: the cell buttons fill their cells and show a focus outline.

REGRESSION TESTS (e2e/accessibility.spec.ts)

  so the audit table has a real row.
